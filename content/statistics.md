This page shows the complete download statistics collected by SourceForge for
the Java Modelling Tools project: totals, downloads over time, operating
systems, countries, and the operating-system-by-country breakdown. The numbers
are fetched from the SourceForge
[Download Stats API](https://sourceforge.net/p/forge/documentation/Download%20Stats%20API/)
at build time and refresh automatically on every deploy.
