JABA applies asymptotic bound techniques to identify the bottlenecks and the
asymptotic values of multiple class closed models. Currently only two or
three class models are supported with any number of stations.

Model definition is guided with a *wizard* interface; identified bottlenecks
are presented both in textual and graphical layout.
