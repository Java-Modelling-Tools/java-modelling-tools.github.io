JMCH (Modelling Classroom Helper) is a teaching tool that applies a simulation
technique to solve a simple model and show the underlying Markov Chain.
Currently support is limited to the following single station models:

- Finite queue (M/M/1/k)
- Infinite queue (M/M/1)

It is also possible to dynamically change the arrival rate and service time of
the system.
